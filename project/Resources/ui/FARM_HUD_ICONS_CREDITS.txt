Farm HUD icon atlas (2026-10-19)

farm_hud_icons.png: project-owned mesh thumbnails, shop packets and procedural art.
Generated offline by tools/ui/generate_farm_hud_icons.py and farm_item_icons.py
using Pillow and NumPy. No additional runtime renderer or dependency is required.
Crop icons use the existing crop_carrot, crop_carrot_leaves, crop_tomato and
crop_pumpkin OBJ geometry, with mature FarmMeshLayout proportions/material colors.
Tomato/pumpkin omit plant foliage, matching the current harvested-fruit effect.
Fixed thumbnail lighting is for readability, not a screenshot of the DX12 pass.
Seed icons use the same packet fronts as the shop (farm_seed_packets*.png);
their existing provenance remains in the respective asset notices.
No world hoe/watering-can meshes exist in the inspected farm resources. Those
tools, the generic seed bag, basket and utility icons retain original UI artwork.
farm_hud_icons.sources.json records the inputs and atlas hash to detect stale art.
No images from Minecraft, Stardew Valley or Farming Simulator are included.
32 cells, 64x64 pixels, 8 columns. Cell order: FarmHUDIcons.h.
